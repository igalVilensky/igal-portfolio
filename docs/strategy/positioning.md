## Editorial principles

1. Show work, not a résumé. Lead with things built and questions explored.
2. Demonstrate engineering thinking through problems, approaches, decisions, trade-offs, limitations, and documented outcomes.
3. Use grounded, precise, human language. A first-person voice and selected personal context are welcome without turning the site into an informal diary or agency pitch.
4. Present AI alongside software engineering, integration, automation, and systems thinking.
5. Keep claims proportional to evidence. Explain what is known; omit or mark uncertain details as requiring confirmation in internal planning.
6. Keep navigation and contact simple. Do not infer openness to jobs or freelance work from the existence of a contact link.
